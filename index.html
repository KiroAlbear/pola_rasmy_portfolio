About

BETWEEN
WORLDS.

I'm Pola Rasmy, an Egyptian filmmaker and visual strategist working across cinema, luxury brands, and global productions.

Camera Assistant on Apple TV+'s Fountain of Youth, directed by Guy Ritchie with John Krasinski and Natalie Portman. Shot at the Pyramids of Giza, Egypt, July 2024.

Cinematic campaigns for Ferrari, Mercedes-Benz, and Lotus. Media PR Manager at King Long Egypt. Founder of Rasmy Studios since 2018.

My work lives between culture and emotion, between continents and frames. I believe stories travel. I build worlds that stay.
